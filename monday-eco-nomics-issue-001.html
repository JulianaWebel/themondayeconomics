Lead story · Regulatory & Policy
Omnibus I is law. Stop waiting and start rebuilding your double materiality.
The simplified ESRS package cuts mandatory datapoints by 61%. Mid-2026 Commission adoption expected, entry into force in Q4. Wave 2 reporters: 2026 is a rebuild year, not a pause year.

The EU Council formally adopted the Omnibus I directive on 24 February, and the simplified ESRS package is now in the Commission's hands. Commissioner Albuquerque has signalled adoption by mid-2026, followed by a scrutiny period, putting entry into force in Q4. Application targets FY2027, with voluntary early adoption for FY2026.

Wave 1 reporters continue under existing rules unless their member state grants exemption. The DMA top-down approach is the rulebook for everyone first-reporting from 2027 onward.

Separately, the Commission published the first official CBAM certificate price: €75.36/tCO₂e, reflecting the average EU ETS closing price for Q1 2026. Importers buy certificates from February 2027 to cover 2026 imports. The free-allocation factor sits at 97.5% this year and falls to zero by 2034.

Why it matters: For German Mittelstand importers in steel, aluminium, and fertilisers, the €75 price point is high enough to materially affect supplier selection. Anyone using default values is overpaying badly.
Sources: EU Council, EFRAG, European Commission

Market & Corporate Moves
Aviation
Delta softens net-zero language as aviation hits physics-and-supply walls

Delta Air Lines reframed its 2050 net-zero target as an "aspiration" on its sustainability webpage and removed references to a 2030 SAF goal, though a spokesperson told ESG Dive the 10% SAF-by-decade-end target remains intact. Sustainable aviation fuel made up just 0.6% of global jet fuel in 2025; IATA expects 0.8% in 2026.

The legal departments are getting nervous about saying "goal" out loud. Delta is not alone in the slow retreat from language that was once presented as commitments. Watch for similar reframings across aviation and heavy industry through Q3.

Sources: ESG Dive, Bloomberg Green

Energi.AI acquires CEMAsys as platform-plus-advisory consolidation continues

Norwegian climate-tech platform Energi.AI announced the acquisition of sustainability advisory firm CEMAsys, quadrupling its customer base and combining AI-driven climate accounting with two decades of advisory practice. Agentic AI is consuming the manual carbon accounting work that consultancies used to bill hourly.

Source: Energi.AI press release
Science & Data Signals
Cryosphere
Glaciers lost 408 Gt in 2025, second-worst year on record

A WGMS-led study published in Nature Reviews Earth and Environment reports that glaciers lost 408 Gt of mass in hydrological year 2025, equivalent to 1.1 mm of sea-level rise. Six of the highest mass-loss years on record have occurred in the past seven years.

Average annual mass loss has nearly quadrupled from under 100 Gt/year in 1976 to 1995, to roughly 390 Gt/year over the past decade. Central Europe, Iceland, and Western Canada led regional losses.

Sources: WGMS, Nature Reviews Earth and Environment, Copernicus C3S/WMO
Climate Modelling
Net-zero pledges still arithmetically reach 2°C, but implementation is the variable

A multi-model analysis published in Nature Climate Change on 1 May finds that if national net-zero pledges are implemented in full, meeting the 2°C target is feasible. Increasing ambition and international cooperation remain crucial.

Read alongside Delta's reframing, that "if implemented" is doing significant work in that sentence.

Source: Nature Climate Change, 1 May 2026
Nature, Biodiversity & Regenerative Economy
Disclosure Frameworks
ISSB confirms nature work as Practice Statement; TNFD adopters keep their advantage

The ISSB confirmed in late April that nature-related guidance will come as a Practice Statement complementing IFRS S1 and S2, not a new mandatory standard. An Exposure Draft is targeted for October 2026 alongside CBD COP17. TNFD completes its technical work by Q3 2026, mirroring the TCFD-into-ISSB transition.

Voluntary adoption now stands at 733 organisations, representing over USD 9 trillion in market capitalisation and USD 22 trillion in assets under management.

Why it matters: TNFD early adopters plug straight into eventual ISSB requirements without rework.
Source: ISSB, TNFD
Climate Finance · Brazil
TFFF remains under-capitalised at $6.7bn against a $25bn target

Brazil's Tropical Forests Forever Facility, launched at COP30, sits at roughly $6.7 billion in pledges against the $25 billion needed. Norway's $3 billion is conditional on sponsor capital reaching $10 billion by end-2026. Germany's contribution is constrained by Bundeshaushalt pressure.

Source: UNFCCC, IISD

Social Impact & Just Transition
Global Energy Diplomacy
Santa Marta closes as a permanent parallel track to COP, not a rival

The first conference on transitioning away from fossil fuels, hosted by Colombia and the Netherlands from 24 to 29 April, brought 57 countries representing roughly a third of the world economy into a new solutions-focused format outside the UNFCCC.

No negotiated text emerged, but a synthesis report is due before COP31.

A science panel was launched and Ireland and Tuvalu confirmed as 2027 co-hosts. Ministers explicitly framed Santa Marta as "complementary to and supportive of the consensus-based, negotiation-focused COP process."

E3G's read: this is now a permanent parallel track where fossil-fuel phase-out planning will get concrete before it reaches multilateral consensus.

Why it matters
For European companies, this is the venue to watch for early signals on fossil-fuel exit timelines, even without direct exposure.
Suppliers and capital providers will be tracking it closely. The planning gets concrete here before multilateral consensus arrives.
Sources: E3G, IISD Earth Negotiations Bulletin, Colombian Government
Tool of the week
European State of the Climate 2025 (Copernicus C3S / WMO)

Released alongside the WGMS glacier study, this report consolidates climate indicators for Europe in one place: temperature anomalies, precipitation, glacier mass balance, fire weather. For anyone preparing CSRD physical-risk narratives or ESRS E1 transition-plan disclosures referencing European climate data, it is the cleanest single source. Free and open access.

The simplified DMA's top-down logic means the worst thing you can do right now is freeze. Pick one material topic, run it through the new approach, and use the rest of Q2 to work out what breaks. The reporters who move now will have a meaningful advantage in 2027.
